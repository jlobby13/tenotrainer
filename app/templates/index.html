Rule-Based Engine

Every clinical decision — stage classification, irritability, dosing, progression — is computed by a deterministic rule engine. No AI makes clinical calls.

Evidence-Grounded

Protocols sourced from peer-reviewed literature: Alfredson (1998), Beyer (2015), Rio (2015), Cook & Purdam (2009), and Gabbett (2016). Citations displayed with every plan.

Adaptive Dosing

Irritability (pain response) modifies how much you load. Stage (loading type) defines what you do. The two axes never interfere with each other.

How It Works

  1. 1
    Complete Onboarding Assessment
    Report pain levels, functional capacity (calf raise reps), injury history, and risk factors.
  2. 2
    Rule Engine Classifies Your Status
    Irritability (High/Moderate/Low) and Rehab Stage (1–3) are determined deterministically from your inputs.
  3. 3
    Receive Your Personalised Plan
    Exercises with calibrated dosing, evidence citations, and an AI-generated plain-English explanation.
  4. 4
    Log Sessions Daily
    Track pain during, after, and next-day. The engine reassesses irritability and decides GO/STAY/CAUTION/STOP.
  5. 5
    Automatic Progression
    Stage progresses only when capacity improves, pain trends down, and you have ≥4 consistent sessions. Never from low pain alone.

Rehab Stages

Stage 1
Capacity Initiation

Isometrics and slow isotonic loading. Builds baseline load tolerance, reduces tendon sensitivity.

Stage 2
Strength Development

Eccentric and heavy slow resistance. Builds tendon structural capacity and strength.

Stage 3
Energy Storage & Release

Plyometrics and reactive strength. Restores elastic function for return to sport.
